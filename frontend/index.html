1. Landscape Analysis: How Sahayata Setu Fills the Operational Gap

While general-purpose voice AI exists (e.g. Manodayam for defence personnel, Vocalysis for hospital stress, and academic SER benchmarks), none are tailored to India's caste-atrocity legal and helpline workflow (NHAA 14566).

2. Auditable Stress Vulnerability Index (SVI) Formula

SVI = Clamp(0, 100, BaseGrievance + ∑ min(Weight_c, Hits_c × Unit_c) + ContextBoost + AcousticModifier)

Lethal Physical Threat (Max 28 pts)

Murder, arson, mob violence, weapons, swords, firearms (Unit: 14 pts/hit)

Restricted Confinement (Max 14 pts)

Barricaded, roads blocked, surrounded, hostage (Unit: 7 pts/hit)

Panic & Extreme Fear (Max 12 pts)

Screaming, crying children, pleading for life (Unit: 6 pts/hit)

Boycott & Discrimination (Max 16 pts)

Water well denial, ration boycott, village ostracization (Unit: 8 pts/hit)

Institutional Pressure (Max 12 pts)

Police refusal of FIR, forced compromise, threats to withdraw (Unit: 6 pts/hit)

Temporal Urgency (Max 14 pts)

Happening right now, breaking in at door (Unit: 7 pts/hit)

Self-Harm & Psychological Crisis Risk (Max 16 pts)

Hopelessness, suicidal ideation, social isolation, fear paralysis (Unit: 8 pts/hit). Triggers independent 24/7 KIRAN referral banner.

🩺 Clinical Framework Alignment: Self-harm and psychological crisis risk categorization is conceptually aligned with structured clinical severity frameworks used in crisis intervention (such as the Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale / C-SSRS), ensuring the scoring approach follows recognized clinical practice rather than ad-hoc keyword matching.

3. Privacy-by-Design & Human-in-the-Loop Ethics

🔒

Zero-PII Storage

Transcripts are processed in-memory. Citizen names and numbers are replaced with synthetic cryptographic aliases (e.g. Citizen_R_71).

🛡️

Consent-Gated Processing

Screening requires affirmative citizen consent. Emergency callers can opt-out to receive standard linear queueing.

👨‍⚖️

Human-in-the-Loop Guarantee

AI suggests priority; trained NHAA duty counsellors and nodal officers must validate every police dispatch or witness protection flag.

📊

Acoustic Confidence Transparency

Explicitly factors in the 45–70% Indian language SER accuracy limitation rather than claiming artificial 100% precision.

🛰️

Vigilance Dashboard

Aggregate case throughput, risk concentration, statutory deadline visibility, and continuous monitoring snapshot.

ℹ️ PoA Rules, 1995 require Vigilance & Monitoring Committees to review case progress only twice yearly (January, July). This dashboard makes that review continuous instead of periodic, using the same underlying case data already captured by this system.
